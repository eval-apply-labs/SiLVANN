## How to accept
Contributions are accepted through pull requests on `github.com/eval-apply-labs/SiLVANN`. The **CLA Assistant**
bot comments on Your first pull request with a link to this Agreement; accepting it there records Your agreement

of an employer or other organisation should contact `admin@evalapply.co.uk` first.
